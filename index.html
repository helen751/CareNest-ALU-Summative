Thriving Together with Confidence

Our goal is to give you the clinical peace of mind you deserve

When your child runs a mild fever, prioritize keeping them hydrated by offering frequent small sips of water, clear broths, or an oral rehydration solution. Dress them in comfortable, breathable cotton fabrics to help heat dissipate naturally, and avoid forcing heavy solid foods if they have a low appetite.

Uninterrupted rest is crucial for recovery. You can improve room comfort by running a cool-mist humidifier in their sleeping area to moisten dry airways. Alternatively, let your child breathe in warm steam from a hot shower in the bathroom before bedtime to help clear nasal congestion.

Always trust your parental intuition. We recommend consulting your healthcare provider directly if your child's fever persists for more than 72 hours, if they exhibit labored or rapid breathing, if they become unusually lethargic and slow to respond, or if they are unable to keep down clear fluids.
